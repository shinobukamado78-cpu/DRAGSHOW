📖 All Stars Competition Formats (reference)

RuPaul’s Drag Race All Stars has used five primary competition formats across its 11 seasons, alongside a few international variants.

Flagship US Formats
Teams / Pairs (AS1)
  • Core Mechanic: Queens form pairs at the start of the season and are judged together.
  • Eliminations: The bottom two teams each send one partner to lip-sync (with a “She-mergency” button allowing the other partner to tag in); both partners are eliminated together.
  • Twists: Teams were only disbanded at the Top 4 finale.
Lip Sync for Your Legacy (AS2, AS3, AS4)
  • Core Mechanic: The top two queens of the week lip-sync against each other for a cash tip.
  • Eliminations: The lip-sync winner pulls a lipstick to eliminate one of the bottom queens.
  • Twists: AS2 featured Revenge of the Queens (eliminated queens returned to win a spot back), and AS3 introduced a Jury of Queers (eliminated queens voted on the Top 2 finalists).
Lip Sync Assassin (AS5, AS6, AS8)
  • Core Mechanic: A single weekly challenge winner lip-syncs against a surprise returning franchise alum (the “Assassin”).
  • Eliminations: If the All-Star wins, she eliminates her chosen bottom queen; if the Assassin wins, a secret group vote by the remaining queens decides who goes home.
  • Twists: AS6 introduced the Game Within a Game (a secret lip-sync tournament among eliminated queens for a spot back), while AS8 featured the Fame Games (a fan-voted runway prize for eliminated queens).
Non-Elimination / Stars & Badges (AS7, AS9)
  • Core Mechanic: Nobody goes home; the top two queens earn a Star (AS7) or Benefactress Badge (AS9) and lip-sync for the power to “block” a rival from earning one the following week.
  • Advancement: The queens with the most Stars/Badges at the end of the season advance to a finale Lip Sync Smackdown.
  • Twists: Used for an all-winners cast (AS7) and a charity season (AS9), featuring late-season double/triple star episodes to let trailing queens catch up.
Bracket Tournament (AS10, AS11)
  • Core Mechanic: An 18-queen cast is split into three separate brackets of six queens competing in three-episode mini-seasons.
  • Advancement: Queens accumulate points from challenge wins and peer-gifted points; top scorers from each bracket (top 3 in AS10; top 2 in AS11) merge into the semi-finals.
  • Eliminations & Finale: Merged episodes revert to traditional Lip Sync for Your Life eliminations (plus a judges’ Wild Card returnee) leading into a finale Lip Sync Smackdown.
